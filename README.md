# KLEOS — AI Search Visibility & Optimization Agent

**NYU SPS × Pulse Foundry Hackathon 2026 | Team Project**

KLEOS is an AI-powered search visibility and optimization prototype designed to help local restaurants understand how they appear in AI-generated recommendations and identify opportunities to improve their digital presence.

**[Explore the Live Application](https://kleos-hsvq.vercel.app/)**

## Project Overview

As consumers increasingly use AI assistants to discover restaurants, businesses need to understand how frequently they are recommended, which competitors appear alongside them, and what factors may affect their visibility.

Our team developed KLEOS to support a four-stage workflow:

**Discovery → Analysis → Action → Measurement**

The project focuses on Café Athena, a Mediterranean restaurant in Pacific Beach, San Diego.

## Key Features

- **AI Visibility Dashboard:** Displays AI mention rates, visibility scores, and share-of-voice indicators.
- **Search Discovery:** Evaluates restaurant visibility across different customer search intents.
- **Competitor Analysis:** Identifies competing restaurants appearing in AI-generated recommendations.
- **Actionable Recommendations:** Highlights opportunities to improve restaurant descriptions, website content, and occasion-based discoverability.
- **Performance Measurement:** Provides a framework for tracking visibility changes over time.

## Research Methodology

We conducted an exploratory AI search visibility analysis using five customer-intent queries tested across ChatGPT and Claude, producing 10 observed responses per restaurant.

The research evaluated:

- AI recommendation frequency
- Relative ranking when mentioned
- Competitor visibility
- Search intent and occasion-based discovery gaps

### Research Findings

| Restaurant | Mentions | Observed Mention Rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Café Athena | 5/10 | 50% |
| Slowly | 6/10 | 60% |
| JRDN | 3/10 | 30% |
| Spitz | 3/10 | 30% |
| Waterbar | 1/10 | 10% |

**Key Insight:** Café Athena performed relatively well in Greek and Mediterranean dining searches but showed opportunities to improve visibility for occasion-based searches, including date nights and group events.

*These results reflect a limited exploratory sample, not overall market share or comprehensive AI search rankings. The live prototype's dashboard uses a separate Perplexity-based scan.*

## Strategic Recommendations

Our analysis identified opportunities to:

1. Strengthen occasion-based restaurant content.
2. Improve website descriptions and FAQs.
3. Clarify dining options, group events, and customer-relevant attributes.
4. Structure business information to improve machine readability.
5. Monitor AI search visibility through repeated measurement.

These recommendations are proposed optimization opportunities rather than experimentally validated improvements.
